biosecurity · ginkgo bioworks · 2020 – 2024

At Ginkgo Bioworks I took five biosecurity products through concept, prototype, field trials, and production — among them aircraft wastewater collection devices deployed at SFO, Boston Logan, and JFK during the COVID-19 pandemic response, and pathogen-surveillance field kits shipped to Ukraine.

I ran R&D and engineering on the triturator collection device (v1 at SFO and Logan, v2 at JFK), designed the device disinfection station, and co-designed the v2 collection device and a passive collection device for low-resource settings. Failure analysis on field returns brought the field failure rate from about 2% to 0.1% in six months.

what I owned

supply chain & manufacturing

Owned the BOMs, materials forecasting, procurement, and production tracking for seven product lines, through prototyping and contract manufacturing. We produced thousands of school testing kits and hundreds of wastewater devices with dozens of vendors.

Owned supplier and materials contracting; wrote and evaluated RFQs and RFPs. Built the assembly workflows, SOPs, and inventory processes that production ran on.

quality & failure analysis

Ran failure analysis on devices that failed in the field, built test rigs, and added verification checks to assembly and refurbishment. The field failure rate fell from about 2% to 0.1% in six months.

traceability

Built automated lot tracking on our barcode system for shipped vials and devices, so we could recall and investigate suspect lots and follow each device’s lifecycle and failures in the field.

Wrote the requirements for the custom tracking system Ginkgo engineering built for the Ukraine deployment: chain of custody, kit performance, refurbishment cycles, and failure modes.

field operations

Proposed decentralized disinfection stations near airports, backed by a cost and risk analysis and a working prototype. The Rwanda airport program used this approach, and it cut turnaround time and cost.

team

For my first two years, managed the kitting team at Concentric by Ginkgo and trained its staff on logistics, production tracking, forecasting, and issue tracking.

the devices

v1

Aircraft wastewater collection device

Developed at the height of the pandemic for governments and airlines running COVID-19 monitoring. I managed a team of engineers to a minimum viable product, then ran development, testing, procurement, manufacturing schedule, and product improvements.

v2

Aircraft wastewater collection device

A second-generation redesign of the collection device. I worked with the cross-functional team that conceived the revamp, and managed testing, procurement, and manufacturing — including the customizable collection tool that attaches to it.

v1 · SFO, Boston Logan

Triturator collection device

Rapidly prototyped from custom and off-the-shelf parts, and deployed with success at San Francisco International and Boston Logan. I ran R&D, engineering, development, procurement, and performance data analytics.

v2 · JFK

Triturator collection device

The second generation, engineered primarily from custom parts. I led the product concept, oversaw engineering, and ran deployment at John F. Kennedy International.

field operations

Airport device disinfection station

Business development needed a way for field operators to disinfect collection devices easily and consistently, and nothing on the market did it. I designed and built the station, and wrote its SOP and quality documentation.

low-resource settings

Passive wastewater collection device

Passively collects target pathogenic material for testing and disease monitoring where active sampling isn’t practical. I worked on R&D, design, and field piloting.
